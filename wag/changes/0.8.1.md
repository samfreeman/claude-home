# 0.8.1 — Every POC says it's a preview

**From:** 0.8.0
**Date:** 2026-10-29

The POC goes to business people who may not know what a POC is. Without being told, they can
reasonably expect what they type to be saved, or that the people and numbers are real. Every
POC now says plainly, on its landing page, what it is.

## What changed

- **The starter's landing page** (`wag/templates/poc/pages/index.html`) has a POC notice: a
  strip marked `data-poc-notice`, directly under the top bar and visible without scrolling:

  > **This is a preview, not the finished product.** It's here so you can see how APP will
  > look and work, and tell us whether it's right before it's built. You can click through
  > every page, but the people, messages and figures are made up, nothing you do is saved, and
  > nothing is sent anywhere.

  The app's name is filled in from `data.js`.
- **Step 5 requires it** (`wag/workflows/poc.md`): keep its meaning and plain language, and
  restyle it to the chosen look, but never remove it.

## Reconcile a 0.8.0 project

- **Detect:** a POC whose `pages/index.html` has no `data-poc-notice` strip.
- **Reconcile:** add the strip from the starter under the landing page's top bar (edit
  `pages/index.html.md` first, then decode).
- **Verify:** the signed-out landing page shows the notice without scrolling.
