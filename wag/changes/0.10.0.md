# 0.10.0 — The POC's backlog is WAG's epic folders, shown as nested accordions

**From:** 0.9.5
**Date:** 2026-10-29

What the VÖROOM POC does now, made the POC command's standard for every project. It reverses
0.9.2's single `.wag/docs/Backlog.md`: there's no fixed shape to parse and nothing to convert
at the handoff.

## What changed

- **`wag/workflows/poc.md`, Step 5:** the backlog is written straight into
  `.wag/backlog/epic-NNN-word/`, with `epic.md` and one `PBI-PPP.md` per PBI, from
  `templates/backlog-epic.md` and `backlog-pbi.md`. PBIs are numbered within their epic and
  referred to as `PBI EEE.PPP`. Epics are `**Status:** Ready`. Epic 000 General keeps its three
  standard PBIs; Epic 501 Future has one PBI per item the PRD defers.
- **`wag/workflows/poc.md`, the docs copies:** the backlog's copies are
  `.wag/poc/docs/backlog/index.js` (`window.POC_BACKLOG = {source, generated, files, epics}`,
  loading each epic file with `document.write`) and one `epic-NNN-word.js` per epic folder
  (pushing `{id, title, folder, markdown, pbis: [{id, title, file, markdown}]}`). Regenerate
  an epic's file whenever anything in its folder changes, and `index.js` whenever an epic is
  added or removed. They're never edited by hand.
- **`wag/workflows/poc.md`, Step 6 and the starter's `pages/project-documents.html`:** the
  Backlog is a three-level accordion (Backlog, each epic with its `epic.md`, each PBI). One
  open per level by the `details` `name` attribute; opening an accordion whose header ended
  up above the screen scrolls it back into view, under the top bar (`--top-bar`, 0 in the
  starter); an open header gets the accent tint and chevron, and each level's content is a
  shade darker than its parent. The backlog has no Download button.
- **`wag/workflows/poc.md`, Step 8:** the handoff copies `.wag/` with the backlog already in
  place; the conversion step is gone.
- **`commands/wag/poc.md`** and **`wag/workflows/init.md`** (Phase D): say the same. The
  command's readable-copy line also catches up with 0.9.5 (HTML always has a sibling; other
  files only if they fail the read-back test).
- **`wag/references/ui-standard.md`:** two house rules for every app: *Nested accordions*
  (one open per level, scroll to what opened, nesting shown by colour) and *Visual changes are
  shown, not described*.

## Reconcile a 0.9.x project

- **Detect:** a `.wag/docs/Backlog.md`, or a `.wag/poc/docs/Backlog.js`.
- **Reconcile:**
  - Split `Backlog.md` into `.wag/backlog/`: each `## Epic EEE — Name` becomes
    `epic-EEE-word/epic.md` headed `# Epic EEE: Name`, with its one-line goal opening
    `## Goal` and `**Status:** Ready` under its priority; each `### PBI EEE.PPP — Title`
    becomes `PBI-PPP.md` in its epic's folder, headed `# PBI EEE.PPP: Title`, with its
    one-line description opening `## Description`; every `####` section becomes `##`.
    Then delete `Backlog.md` and `poc/docs/Backlog.js`.
  - Generate `poc/docs/backlog/index.js` and one file per epic.
  - Replace the page's Backlog item and script with the starter's (`pages/project-documents.html`),
    keeping the project's own look.
- **Verify:** Project Documents shows every epic and PBI; opening one closes its siblings at
  that level; the levels are told apart by colour in both modes.
