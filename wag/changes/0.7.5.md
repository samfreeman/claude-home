# 0.7.5 — The POC starter copy no longer overwrites the project theme

**From:** 0.7.4
**Date:** 2026-10-28

Found by voice. In `/wag:poc`, Step 4 writes the project's theme to `.wag/poc/theme.css`,
taken from the chosen mockup. Step 5 then said to copy `~/.claude/wag/templates/poc/` into
`.wag/poc/`, and that folder has its own neutral `theme.css`. Copied literally, it replaced
the project's theme with the starter's.

## What changed

Step 5 (`wag/workflows/poc.md`) now lists what to copy:

- **Copy:** `index.html` (read through `index.html.md`, `‹›` turned back into `<>`),
  `data.js`, `vercel.json`, `.vercelignore`.
- **Don't copy:** `theme.css` (Step 4's stays), nor the starter's sync-generated
  `index.html.md` and `data.js.md`. Those use claude-home's note-plus-fence format, so the
  project writes its own `index.html.md` under the readable-copy rule.

## Reconcile a 0.7.4 project

- **Detect:** a POC whose `.wag/poc/theme.css` still starts with `tweakcn: custom` and holds
  the shadcn neutral defaults, while the chosen mockup has its own colours.
- **Reconcile:** write the chosen mockup's `:root` and `.dark` blocks back into
  `.wag/poc/theme.css` (Step 4). Delete any copied-over `data.js.md`, and replace a copied
  `index.html.md` with a fresh readable copy of the project's `index.html`.
- **Verify:** the POC renders in the chosen look's colours in both light and dark.
