# 0.10.1 — Backlog accordion fixes: headers stand out, titles show once

**From:** 0.10.0
**Date:** 2026-10-30

Two things seen on VÖROOM's deployed POC:
- Closed PBI headers had no fill of their own, so they were the same colour as the epic
  content they sit in, and only a thin border showed where each PBI started.
- An open epic or PBI showed its title twice: in the accordion's header, and again as the
  file's `# ` heading at the top of its content.

## What changed

- **Starter `pages/project-documents.html`:**
  - Closed epic and PBI headers are filled (`level-2-head`, `level-3-head`) with the colour
    they sit on mixed 8% toward `--foreground`: lighter in dark mode, darker in light. The
    level shades are now the variables `--level-2` and `--level-3`. Open headers still take
    the accent.
  - Epics and PBIs render through `body()`, which drops the file's leading `# ` title line.
    The files keep their titles.
- **`wag/workflows/poc.md`** (Step 6) and **`wag/references/ui-standard.md`** (*Nested
  accordions*): a closed header never matches what it sits on (a border alone isn't enough),
  and an accordion's content doesn't repeat its header's title.

## Reconcile a 0.10.0 project

- **Detect:** Project Documents' epic or PBI `summary` elements have no background of their
  own, or an open epic or PBI starts with its title again.
- **Reconcile:** from the starter page, add the `--level-2`/`--level-3` variables, the
  `.level-2-head`/`.level-3-head` rules (with those classes on the epic and PBI `summary`
  elements) and `body()` (used for each epic's and PBI's markdown), in the project's own
  theme.
- **Verify:** with an epic open, its closed PBI headers stand out from the epic's content in
  both light and dark, and neither the epic nor an open PBI repeats its title.
